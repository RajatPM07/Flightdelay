Real-time flight status lookup

Enter a flight number — the engine queries both vendors and reconciles them into one verdict (vendors often disagree on freshness; the in-house brain resolves it). Use “One feed” to inspect a single provider.

Edge cases handled by the engine

These are covered in code. Click “try it” to see the real behaviour.

IATA → ICAO / spacing

Ticket ident (6E1341) maps to FlightAware ICAO (IGO1341); AeroDataBox’s spaced form is normalised.

Flight not found

A bogus ident returns a clean message, not a crash or empty screen.

Future-date / rate-limit

FlightAware ~2-day future limit (400) and AeroDataBox ~1 req/s (429) surface readable messages.

Gate vs runway / reconciliation

Delay is computed from the best gate-arrival estimate (not runway touchdown); both feeds reconcile to one status.
