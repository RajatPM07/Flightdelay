Features

Built with the right guardrails

Every design decision in TripSecure+ exists for a reason — no magic numbers, no data slop, no unnecessary noise.

Three configurable delay tiers

Thresholds aren't hardcoded. T1, T2, T3 — and the backstop close window — are all config values. We only alert on tier crossings, not on every ping.

T1 · 30 min T2 · 60 min T3 · 120 min

Multi-channel instant alerts

WhatsApp via Twilio, SMS fallback, and email via SendGrid — all triggered simultaneously on a notifiable transition, with full idempotency.

PII isolation by design

Only the policy_pii table holds name, phone, and email. Every other table and log references only the anonymised policy ID — DPDP §6 compliant from day one.

Vendor-agnostic brain

The decision engine is pure Python — no SDK, DB, or httpx inside. Swap FlightAware for any other data feed and the brain behaves identically. Future-proof by design.

FAQ

Common questions

Which flights are eligible?+
Any flight enrolled under ICICI Lombard Product 4233 (TripSecure+). You'll need the PNR and flight number from your booking confirmation.
When exactly do I get notified?+
At three thresholds: T1 (30 min delay), T2 (60 min), T3 (120 min). Also on recovery (back to on-time), departure with a new ETA, landing, cancellation, and diversion. Never on every radar ping — only on meaningful state changes.
What does TripSecure+ do when my flight is delayed?+
It sends an instant WhatsApp, SMS, and email the moment your delay crosses a threshold — and again on departure and landing. TripSecure+ is a monitoring and notification service only; anything beyond alerts is handled separately by ICICI Lombard.
Is my personal data safe?+
Yes. Your name, phone, and email are stored in an isolated PII table. All flight tracking and decision logs reference only your anonymised policy ID — fully DPDP Act §6 compliant. Data is used only for flight monitoring.
What if my flight is cancelled or diverted?+
You'll be notified immediately when the state changes to CANCELLED or DIVERTED. Monitoring continues until the flight reaches a terminal state or the 24-hour backstop closes the record.
What if I miss the WhatsApp message?+
We send notifications via WhatsApp, SMS, and optionally email simultaneously. If one channel fails we retry. You can also log back into the demo portal to see the full event timeline at any time.
